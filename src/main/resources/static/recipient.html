Welcome, Recipient!

FeastForward connects you with nearby donors offering surplus food. Whether you're an individual or part of a community organization, you can request fresh, safe meals with ease.

How to Request Food

1. Sign In

Log in to your account to access available listings.

2. Browse Listings

View food donations near your location and check availability.

3. Submit Request

Choose what you need and submit a request for pickup or delivery.
