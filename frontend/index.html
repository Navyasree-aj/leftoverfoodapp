Connecting Surplus Food with Those in Need

FeastForward helps restaurants, grocery stores, and event organizers share leftover food with recipients and communities — reducing waste and fighting hunger.

About FeastForward

Every year, tons of food goes to waste while many people go hungry. FeastForward bridges this gap by connecting donors, recipients, and volunteers to ensure surplus food reaches the right hands. Together, we contribute to UN SDG 2: Zero Hunger.

How It Works

1. Donors List Food

Restaurants, events, and groceries upload surplus food details.

2. Recipients Request

Individuals or NGOs request available food nearby.

3. Volunteers Deliver

Trained volunteers handle pickups and deliveries.
